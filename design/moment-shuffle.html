Things to try
  • Tap Shuffle.
  • Press and hold Shuffle to wind it up, then let go for a faster spin.
  • Drag or fling a blob. It drifts back home.
  • Tap a blob to pick that moment yourself.
  • Hit Reroll on the result to spin again.
